# Customer Approval → Publish / Schedule
> id: customer_approval, publish_schedule · stage: 12–13 · engine: human + Instagram Content Publishing API

## Role
- **Customer Approval**: the customer reviews the numbers, the strategy and the QC'd briefs and
  approves the report. Nothing is created or published before this.
- **Publish / Schedule**: approved drafts are edited in the Editor and published through the
  Instagram Content Publishing API (feed, reel, carousel, story) at the strategy's posting slot.
